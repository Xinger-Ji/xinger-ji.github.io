Eldercare Survey Analysis

Turning community survey records into demand patterns and practical plans for age-friendly services.

OrganizationShanxi Youran Smart Elderly Care
My roleElderly Care Services Project Coordinator
DateMarch–August 2025
OverviewMy workMethodsDeliverables

Overview

Home-based care, assistive-device rental, and age-friendly home modifications meet different needs. The project connected community research with the practical requirements of service planning and government-facing proposals.

My work

I led four interns researching care needs and service-delivery models. I analyzed 2,632 community survey records by age, gender, and spending level, and authored 12 technical proposals for elderly-care service projects.

  • Survey analysis
  • Segmentation
  • Market research
  • Technical proposals

Methods

  1. Define service questions

    Research home-based care, assistive-device rental, home modification, and community service delivery.

  2. Compare community needs

    Analyze survey records by age, gender, and spending level to identify demand patterns.

  3. Connect findings to planning

    Translate those patterns into service-planning and subsidy recommendations.

  4. Make the proposals usable

    Write formal implementation plans for public-sector review and collaboration.

Deliverables

The deliverables connected community demand analysis with service recommendations and 12 technical proposals for public-sector review.

Source artifacts & scope +

The survey data are not included. No individual-level records or invented segment percentages are displayed. The findings describe patterns in the surveyed community and do not establish causal effects.
