NCAA Tournament Modeling

Overview

A seed gives a useful starting point, but it does not describe everything about a team. For the 2026 Brandeis Datathon, I worked with NCAA tournament data to investigate upset patterns and compare ways to predict tournament outcomes.

My work

I integrated matchup, seed, team-metric, ranking, and résumé data from a 40+ CSV archive. My work included exploratory analysis, feature engineering, model comparison, and Monte Carlo simulation.

  • Python
  • EDA
  • Feature engineering
  • Monte Carlo simulation

Methods

  1. Bring the data together

    Combine the core tournament datasets covering 2008–2025 so matchups can be considered alongside seeds and team-level information.

  2. Explore performance

    Examine upset patterns and seed or team overperformance through exploratory analysis.

  3. Compare models

    Engineer predictive features and compare modeling approaches for tournament outcomes.

  4. Represent uncertainty

    Use Monte Carlo simulation to examine possible tournament paths rather than treating one bracket as certain.

Deliverables

The project produced analysis of historical tournament patterns and predictions for the 2026 tournament. The workflow connected data integration, feature engineering, model comparison, and simulation.
