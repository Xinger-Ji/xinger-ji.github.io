02 / ABOUT ME

A little context.

about.txt− □ ×

As an MS Business Analytics candidate at Brandeis, I’ve worked on predictive modeling, survey analysis, and the evaluation of business tools and workflows. I enjoy moving between the technical work and its practical implications: comparing approaches, explaining tradeoffs, and developing recommendations a team can put into practice.

My undergraduate background is in International Affairs. It taught me to examine how institutions, policies, and people shape an outcome—a perspective I bring to understanding the context behind the data and communicating findings to different audiences.

Beyond analytics, I’m a program director and board member at Workers’ Assist, contributing to community projects alongside my work in adult English education. I also make time for photography, with a particular interest in the places, people, and small details I encounter.
