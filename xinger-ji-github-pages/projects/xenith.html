Xenith Robotics Website & Product Work

Building a company website and translating three robot forms into clear materials for customers and partners.

OrganizationXenith Robotics
My roleMarketing / Product Strategy Intern
DateJune–August 2026
OverviewMy workMethodsDeliverables

Overview

Xenith was preparing desktop companion, reception, and wheeled home-service robots for commercialization. The work required moving between engineering details, audience needs, web implementation, and external partnership requirements.

My work

I built responsive product and cooperation pages, developed audience-specific messaging and visuals, designed surveys for three stakeholder groups, and prepared applications for three music platforms. I also explored early hardware and screen-interface concepts.

  • Next.js
  • HTML/CSS
  • Git / GitHub
  • Tencent Cloud
  • Nginx
  • SolidWorks

Methods

  1. Build and explain

    Develop the company website in Next.js with responsive layouts, product pages, navigation, and partner-facing content.

  2. Work through deployment constraints

    Test a mainland-accessible deployment path using Tencent Cloud and Nginx when the initial Cloudflare-hosted version was unreliable for the intended audience.

  3. Structure stakeholder feedback

    Design surveys for public, government or eldercare, and industry audiences to turn broad product ideas into comparable questions.

  4. Translate product requirements

    Research licensing requirements for voice-controlled music playback and explore early wrist-interface and screen-animation concepts.

Deliverables

The website returned successfully from the mainland server, and the company’s ICP filing was approved in July 2026. Other outputs included exhibition materials, survey instruments, licensing application packages, product visuals, and early concepts for engineering discussion.

Source artifacts & scope +

Licensing applications were preparation work, not confirmed approvals or completed integrations. The wrist interface was an early concept, not a tested production design. Website traffic, sales, and product shipments are not claimed.
