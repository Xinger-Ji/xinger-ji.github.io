Valusync Platform Evaluation

Evaluating five platforms and designing a repeatable content workflow for a mission-driven fintech company.

OrganizationValusync × Brandeis International Business School
My roleBusiness Analytics Field Project Consultant · team project
DateJune–August 2026
OverviewMy workMethodsDeliverables

Overview

The field project focused on social-media execution for Valusync. A platform recommendation needed to account for the content process around the software, including organization, tracking, and a routine for reviewing results.

My work

I evaluated five social-media management platforms across three testing rounds, translating client priorities into a structured scoring framework. I designed a master CSV, a tagging taxonomy, a UTM structure, and a weekly review workflow, and presented recommendations with implementation-ready handoff materials.

  • Evaluation framework
  • CSV design
  • Tagging taxonomy
  • UTM structure

Methods

  1. Translate priorities into criteria

    Build a structured scoring framework around the client’s practical requirements.

  2. Test the shortlisted platforms

    Evaluate five tools across three rounds of testing and compare their fit.

  3. Design the information structure

    Create a master CSV, content tags, and a UTM structure to keep content and tracking organized.

  4. Plan the handoff

    Deliver recommendations and a weekly review workflow the client can use after the project.

Deliverables

The team delivered a recommendation and implementation-ready handoff materials. My contribution connected the platform evaluation with the data structure and review routine needed to use the recommendation.
